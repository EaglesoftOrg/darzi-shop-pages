Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026 · Operator: EagleSoftOrg

This policy describes how Darzi Shop handles information entered into the app. For privacy questions, contact darzishophelp@gmail.com.

Information handled

  • Account email addresses and authentication information for sign in, password recovery and staff access.
  • Shop name, telephone numbers, address and staff invitations.
  • Customer names, telephone numbers, clothing measurements, orders, delivery status, payment amounts and notes entered by the shop.
  • Local copies of shop data and pending changes stored on the device so the app can work offline.

The app records payment amounts; it does not ask for payment card details. Please do not enter card details or other unnecessary sensitive information in notes.

Why the information is used

Darzi Shop uses this information to manage shop accounts, staff access, customers, measurements, orders, printable slips, payments, offline synchronization and shop statistics. The shop owner and invited staff can access records for their shop according to their permissions.

Storage and service providers

Account and shop information is stored with Supabase, the app's authentication and database provider. A local copy is stored in the app's private device storage for offline use and synchronizes when internet access returns. Brevo processes the sender and recipient email addresses and delivery information needed to send authentication emails, such as password reset messages. A PDF slip shared or printed using your device goes to the destination you choose.

Data is transmitted to these services over encrypted connections. Access to shop records is restricted to signed in shop members; please protect access to your device and account. EagleSoftOrg does not sell shop or customer records.

Retention and deletion

Active account and shop records are kept while the account and shop are in use. An owner deleting their account in the app removes their owned shop records, including customers, measurements, orders and payments, from the active database. A staff member deleting their account removes their account and access; records of a shop owned by someone else remain with that shop.

Open Settings → Account delete karein in the app, or request account deletion without the app. A request sent by email may require verification that you control the account. Copies already shared outside the app, such as printed or exported slips, are controlled by their recipients. Service providers may retain temporary operational copies under their own backup and security retention practices. If a legal obligation requires retention of particular records, we will explain this when responding to a deletion request.

Your questions and choices

To ask about access, correction or deletion of information, email darzishophelp@gmail.com. Shop owners are responsible for informing their customers about the measurements and contact information they enter into the app.
