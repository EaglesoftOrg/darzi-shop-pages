Darzi Shop

Delete your Darzi Shop account

Darzi Shop is operated by EagleSoftOrg. You can request deletion even if you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in.

Request deletion without the app

  1. Send an email to darzishophelp@gmail.com with the subject Darzi Shop account deletion.
  2. State the email address used for your Darzi Shop account. Do not send your password or customer records.
  3. We will verify that you control the account, then process the request and reply to you.

Email deletion request

If the button does not open your email app, copy darzishophelp@gmail.com and send the request directly. You do not need to reinstall the app.

Delete in the app

Sign in, open Settings → Account delete karein, then confirm. Sync pending offline changes before deleting the account.

What is removed

Deleting an owner account removes that account and its owned shops from the active service, including associated customers, measurements, orders and payment records. Deleting a staff account removes that account and its access; business records owned by another shop owner remain. Exported or printed slips already shared outside the app cannot be removed from recipients' devices. Temporary service provider backups and information that must be retained for a legal obligation may remain for the applicable retention period; we will explain any such retention when handling your request.
